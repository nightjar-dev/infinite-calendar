An independent extension by nightjar

Your calendar.
Less rigidity.

Infinite Scrolling for Google Calendar™ replaces the paginated Month view with a continuous scrolling calendar grid.

Improve your workflow.

Browse your existing calendars in one scrolling view. Show or hide calendars, see events across multiple days, and open crowded days to see the rest.

Create, edit, move, copy, and delete events, with changes saved to Google Calendar.

Your calendars stay with Google.

Google handles sign-in. Calendar requests travel directly between your browser and Google; the developer does not operate a server that receives your calendar records or authorization tokens.

No advertising or analytics in the extension. Read the privacy policy for storage, permissions, and deletion details.

How to use

  1. Install the extension.
  2. Open Google Calendar in Month view and enable Infinite Calendar from the extension popup.
  3. Connect your Google account and approve Calendar access.
  4. Scroll. Switch the extension off whenever you want the native view back.

A focused Month view

The extension currently supports Google Calendar’s English interface. Other views remain native, and some advanced Google Calendar features are not available in the custom view.

It requests permission to read your calendar list and view and edit events. It does not request access to your Gmail inbox or your Google password.
